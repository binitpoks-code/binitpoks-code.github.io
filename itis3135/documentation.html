Choosing a Fragrance

Try a small sample before buying a full bottle. Give it time to settle, since the scent can change as you wear it.

Fragrance Types

Fresh, woody, sweet, citrus, spicy, and aquatic are useful starting points. A fragrance can fit more than one type.

How Many Sprays

Start with one spray and see how strong it feels. Use less in shared spaces and follow the product directions.

Where to Apply

Follow the label when applying fragrance. Avoid your eyes and irritated skin, and stop using it if it causes irritation.

Storage Tips

Keep bottles closed and away from heat and direct sunlight. A cool cupboard is a better spot than a sunny windowsill.
