# Product Image Provenance

Seven original fictional product visuals were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this site. They depict product concepts, not photographs of manufactured stock. No stock photographs or real models were used.

The hero is 1200 ? 800. Each product image is 720 ? 900. Files were resized with containment to preserve the complete products and encoded as WebP at quality 82. The complete photo set is approximately 260 KB. The favicon is an original code-native SVG BP monogram.
