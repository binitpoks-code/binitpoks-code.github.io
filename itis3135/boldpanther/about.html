About

Independent by Design

Built Around Real Days

Bold Panther Sportswear began as a fictional Charlotte-based brand idea by Binit Pokharel: bring the ease of a favorite sports shirt into a whole wardrobe. The starting point was not a podium. It was the everyday rhythm of classes, a pickup game, a walk, and time with friends.

The Bold Panther stands for showing up as yourself. Our audience includes casual sports fans, competitive players, students, and people who simply like the feel of athletic clothing. No fitness level, body type, or personal best is a condition of entry.

We keep the concept collection intentionally small. A jersey with character, a coordinated tracksuit, familiar essentials, and a few practical accessories make a more useful starting point than an endless catalog.
