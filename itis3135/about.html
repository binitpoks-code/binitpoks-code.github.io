About Me

I’m a Computer Science student at UNC Charlotte with a strong interest in software engineering, backend development, data, and open-source development. I enjoy building things and understanding how they actually work instead of just getting them to run.

Outside of my coursework, I’ve worked on projects that have pushed me to learn technologies we haven’t really covered much in school, including TypeScript, Apache Kafka, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, authentication, and other backend and data tools. I also enjoy exploring and contributing to open-source projects, with one of my more recent contributions being to pandas.

Outside of technology, fitness and lifting are a big part of my life. I also enjoy hiking, traveling, and being outdoors. I’m pretty focused on progress and self-improvement, whether that’s with programming, school, or training.

My goal is to be able to take something from “I have no idea how this works” to “I built it, I understand it, and I can explain why it works.”
